    Map tips

    A few moves that aren’t obvious at first.

    Shift + drag
    Draw a box to zoom straight into it
    Ctrl + drag
    Rotate and tilt the map (or drag with the right mouse button)
    Double-click
    Zoom in — add Shift to zoom out
    Scroll / pinch
    Zoom in and out

    Keyboard

    ←↑→↓
    Pan
    Shift + arrows
    Rotate (← →) and tilt (↑ ↓)
    + −
    Zoom — hold Shift to jump further
    Pinch
    Zoom in and out
    Two fingers, twist
    Rotate the map
    Two fingers, drag up/down
    Tilt into 3D
    Double-tap
    Zoom in
